StoryQuest

Printable activity worksheets to accompany any book your child (or you!) is reading. An AI writes the pages; this page lays them out for printing.

Free. Everything happens on this page in your browser: nothing you paste is uploaded. Your last packet is kept on this device only, so you can come back to it.

Showing the last packet made on this device.

1Copy the instructions for your AI

Press the button, then paste into any chat AI you use: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot. Free versions work. Ones that can search the web get the book's details more accurate. The AI will ask your child's first name and age; a nickname is fine.

Show the instructions instead

2Answer its questions

The AI asks about the book, your child and how many pages you want. Then it writes everything as one long block of text.

Most AIs put a Copy button on that block. Use it so you get every bit. If the AI stops partway, type continue, then copy both parts and paste them together.

Printing tips
  • To keep a PDF, choose Save as PDF as the printer.
  • Leave Margins on Default and Scale at 100%.
  • Turn on Background graphics (Chrome, Edge) or Print backgrounds (Safari) so the colours print. Or pick the Clean look to save ink.
  • Each quest is exactly one sheet. The answer guide is usually two or three.
  • On a phone or tablet: a laptop is easiest. On an iPhone or iPad, tap Print, then pinch outward on the preview, then Share, then Save to Files to keep a PDF. On Android, choose Save as PDF as the printer.
